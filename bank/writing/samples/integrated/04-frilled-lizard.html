04 · 伞蜥的"领圈"干嘛用 · 综合写作范文

主题：动物行为 / 功能解释 ｜ 难度：★★☆☆☆ ｜ 字数：~275

题目

Reading（阅读） 文章认为伞蜥（frilled lizard）颈部那圈皮膜用于体温调节。解释：皮膜展开后增大体表表面积，可在清晨吸收更多阳光升温，或在过热时通过对流散热；这是爬行动物常见的"辐射器"结构。

Listening（听力） 讲座反对，主张领圈的主功能是威慑捕食者。教授说明：受威胁时，蜥蜴猛然竖起领圈、张开大嘴、发出嘶声，瞬间显得大了一圈，吓退猛禽与蛇；体温调节其实靠行为（晒太阳姿势、躲阴凉）完成，与领圈无关。领圈在求偶展示中也被使用，进一步说明它是"信号器官"而非散热器。

范文

The reading passage claims that the frilled lizard's neck membrane functions in thermoregulation. The lecture, however, challenges this, arguing that the frill mainly serves as a defense display.

First, the reading suggests the frill's large surface area helps the lizard absorb morning sun or release heat by convection. The professor counters that temperature control is achieved through behavior—basking posture and shade-seeking—not through the membrane, so the thermoregulation role is overstated.

Second, while the passage treats the frill as a radiator, the lecture shows its real trigger is threat. When alarmed, the lizard erects the frill, opens its mouth, and hisses, suddenly appearing much larger and intimidating predators such as birds of prey and snakes.

Finally, the reading ignores the frill's use in courtship. The speaker points out that males display the frill to rivals and mates, which confirms its primary identity as a signaling organ rather than a cooling device.

Thus, the lecture undermines the reading's thermoregulation-centered interpretation.

中文解析

结构拆解

  • 开头：阅读"体温调节" ↔ 讲座"威慑/信号"，功能之争。
  • 三段：体温靠行为而非领圈 / 受威胁时的威慑机制 / 求偶展示佐证"信号器官"。
  • 收尾点明"以体温为中心的解释被推翻"。

亮点表达 / 句型

  • functions in thermoregulation（function in + 名词，学术写法）
  • overstated（"被夸大"，比 wrong 更准，体现程度）
  • primary identity as a signaling organ（primary identity 精准概括"本质功能"）

写作思路（可借鉴点）

  1. 动物行为类常考"某结构的功能之争"：阅读给一个功能，讲座给更可能的另一个 + 行为替代解释。套 achieved through behavior, not through X。
  2. 用 trigger（触发条件）区分"何时用"：领圈在受威胁时而非恒温时展开。
  3. 求偶/展示用途常是讲座的"第三点"，用来证明"信号功能"——记 confirms its primary identity as …。
