02 · 象牙喙啄木鸟是否灭绝 · 综合写作范文

主题：动物保护 / 证据可靠性 ｜ 难度：★★☆☆☆ ｜ 字数：~280

题目

Reading（阅读） 文章断言象牙喙啄木鸟已灭绝。理由：自 1944 年后再无确证目击；其赖以生存的南方原始老龄林被大规模砍伐；多次官方搜寻（含架设自动相机）一无所获；与其亲缘相近、生态位相似的几种啄木鸟也已消失，说明该谱系整体崩溃。

Listening（听力） 讲座反驳，认为该鸟很可能仍存活。教授列举：近年在阿肯色与路易斯安那沼泽，多名有经验的观鸟者独立报告目击，描述与象牙喙特有的"双敲"鸣声吻合；录音设备捕获到该独特叫声；目击者均为训练有素的专家，误认概率低。此外，部分河岸林正在恢复，为其提供了潜在栖息地。

范文

The reading passage concludes that the ivory-billed woodpecker is extinct. The lecture, however, contradicts this conclusion and argues that the bird likely still survives.

First, the reading points out that no confirmed sighting has occurred since 1944. The professor pushes back by citing multiple independent reports from experienced observers in Arkansas and Louisiana swamps in recent years, whose descriptions match the species' distinctive features—testimony the passage dismisses too quickly.

Second, while the passage notes that exhaustive searches with automatic cameras found nothing, the lecture highlights audio recordings of the bird's unique "double-knock" call. This acoustic evidence, the speaker argues, is harder to fake than a fleeting visual and strengthens the case for survival.

Finally, the reading claims the loss of old-growth forest sealed the species' fate, and that related woodpeckers have also vanished. The lecture counters that some riparian forests are recovering, offering potential habitat, and that the extinction of relatives does not prove this one is gone.

Thus, the lecture undermines the reading's claim of certain extinction.

中文解析

结构拆解

  • 开头：阅读"已灭绝" ↔ 讲座"仍存活"，直接对立。
  • 三段：目击证词 / 录音证据 / 栖息地恢复，逐点接住阅读的三个论据。
  • 收尾：复用 undermines 收口。

亮点表达 / 句型

  • concludes that … / contradicts this conclusion（结论动词轮换，避免总用 says）
  • testimony the passage dismisses too quickly（点出阅读"草率否定"，是高分写法）
  • harder to fake than a fleeting visual（对比结构，突出证据强度）

写作思路（可借鉴点）

  1. 当阅读用"没找到=不存在"推理时，讲座常从"证据方法不可靠/有新证据"反击——套用 the passage overlooks …。
  2. 写"专家证词"时用 experienced observers / trained experts，呼应听力强调的"可信度"。
  3. 别把阅读当事实复读；每段都要落到"讲座怎么撼动它"。
