01 · 恐龙灭绝：小行星撞击 vs 火山喷发 · 综合写作范文

主题：古生物 / 因果论证 ｜ 难度：★★★☆☆ ｜ 字数：~290

题目

Reading（阅读） 文章认为，约 6600 万年前白垩纪末大灭绝（恐龙消失）由小行星撞击引发。证据是墨西哥尤卡坦半岛的希克苏鲁伯陨石坑，其年代与灭绝层吻合；撞击激起的尘埃与硫化物遮蔽阳光，导致全球骤冷、植物光合作用停止，食物链随之崩塌。文章称这一"瞬间事件"能解释为何海洋与陆地物种几乎同时消失。

Listening（听力） 讲座反对该结论，主张大规模火山喷发（印度德干暗色岩）才是主因。教授指出：德干火山活动持续了数百万年，喷出的二氧化碳与有害气体造成长期气候变暖与海洋酸化，足以逐步抹去物种；灭绝在地层中是渐变而非突变的。此外，灭绝层中的铱元素也可来自火山而非仅限小行星；而陨石坑的形成时间与物种大量死亡的时间并不严格对齐。

范文

The reading passage argues that the mass extinction of dinosaurs was caused by a sudden asteroid impact. However, the lecture challenges this view, attributing the extinction to prolonged volcanic activity instead.

First, the reading claims that the Chicxulub asteroid impact, by throwing dust and sulfur into the atmosphere, triggered instant global cooling and collapsed the food chain. The professor counters that the Deccan Traps volcanism lasted millions of years and released massive CO₂ and toxic gases, producing long-term warming and ocean acidification—a slow poison far more capable of erasing species than a single flash event.

Second, while the passage points to the iridium-rich layer as proof of an asteroid, the lecture notes that volcanism can also deposit iridium. In other words, the very "smoking gun" the reading relies on is not unique to impacts.

Finally, the reading describes the extinction as sudden and simultaneous across land and sea. The speaker demonstrates that the fossil record shows a gradual decline, and that the crater's timing does not line up tightly with the die-off. This undermines the claim that one impact explains everything.

In sum, the lecture casts serious doubt on the reading's asteroid-centered explanation.

中文解析

结构拆解

  • 开头段：一句话点明关系——阅读说小行星，讲座说火山，方向相反。
  • 主体三段：严格「阅读点 ↔ 讲座反驳」一一对应（冷却机制 / 铱证据 / 突变vs渐变）。
  • 收尾：一句总结"讲座质疑阅读"，不啰嗦。

亮点表达 / 句型

  • challenges this view, attributing … to …（反驳 + 归因，一句两用）
  • the very "smoking gun" the reading relies on（smoking gun = 铁证，学术写作加分比喻）
  • casts serious doubt on（综合写作收尾万能句）

写作思路（可借鉴点）

  1. 综合写作第一句就必须亮关系，别写"阅读讲了A，听力讲了B"两截。
  2. 三段对应时，用 First / Second / Finally 显式分段，阅卷人一眼看到三点齐全。
  3. 听力细节要写具体（"持续数百万年""CO₂与有害气体""渐变地层"），这比复述阅读得分高。
