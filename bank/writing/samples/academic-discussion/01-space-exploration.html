01 · 政府该投资太空还是解决地球问题？· 学术讨论写作范文

主题：公共政策 / 科技优先级 ｜ 难度：★★★☆☆ ｜ 目标字数：~155

题目

Professor: Some argue that with so many pressing problems on Earth—poverty, climate change, disease—governments should stop funding space exploration and redirect that money. Do you think public money for space is justified, or should it be spent on Earth? Why?

Claire (student): I lean toward solving Earth problems first. We have people who can't get clean water today, so space feels like a luxury we can't afford.

范文

I'd have to disagree with Claire a bit—I think space funding is justified, as long as it runs alongside—not instead of—solving Earth problems.

The main reason is that space investment produces spin-off technologies we rely on daily. Take satellite imaging and weather forecasting as an example: they were born from space programs yet now help us track storms, manage crops, and even respond to climate disasters. That shows the boundary between "space" and "Earth" isn't as sharp as it seems.

Admittedly, clean water and poverty are urgent, and I'm not saying we should abandon them. But the choice isn't either-or. A small fraction of budgets on space yields tools that ultimately make Earth problems easier to tackle.

Unlike Claire, who frames space as a luxury, I'd argue it's a long-term investment whose returns circle back to life on Earth.

中文解析

结构拆解

  • 第 1 句亮立场：礼貌反驳 Claire，主张"双轨"。
  • 理由 1 + 例子：卫星/气象预报的溢出效益（具体、贴科技背景）。
  • 让步：承认地球问题紧急，但强调"非此即彼"是伪命题。
  • 呼应同学收尾：把"奢侈品"重构为"长期投资"。

亮点表达 / 句型

  • I'd have to disagree with Claire a bit（礼貌反驳同学，讨论感强）
  • spin-off technologies（溢出/衍生技术，科技类万能词）
  • the choice isn't either-or（非此即彼，加分逻辑词）

写作思路（可借鉴点）

  1. 学术讨论第一句就接教授问题+同学立场，别另起炉灶。
  2. 用"具体例子"撑理由——卫星/气象是理工科申请者信手拈来的真例子，比空说"technology helps"得分高。
  3. 让步段 Admittedly … but … 显成熟，是 4→5 的分水岭。
