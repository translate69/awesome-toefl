05 · 招有经验的人还是培养新人？· 学术讨论写作范文

主题：人力资源管理 ｜ 难度：★★★☆☆ ｜ 目标字数：~155

题目

Professor: When building a team, is it better to hire experienced workers at higher cost, or to hire young, trainable people at lower cost? What would you advise a new manager?

Claire (student): Always experienced—they hit the ground running and you don't waste time training.

范文

I'd push back on Claire a little—I'd advise a mixed roster rather than experienced-only.

The reason is that the two groups cover different needs. Experienced hires, as Claire says, deliver immediately and stabilize tough projects. But a good case in point from my lab: when we brought in two junior students and trained them on our signal-processing pipeline, they grew into exactly the roles we needed—at a fraction of the cost, and with deeper loyalty to the team.

Admittedly, a new manager under deadline pressure should lean experienced to avoid risk. Still, an all-veteran team gets expensive and can resist fresh methods.

Unlike Claire, who defaults to seniority, I'd stress balancing both: seniors for stability, juniors for growth and renewal.

中文解析

结构拆解

  • 第 1 句：礼貌反驳 Claire，主张"混合梯队"。
  • 理由 + 例子：两类人互补；用实验室培养新人的真实经历证明"低成本+高忠诚"。
  • 让步：赶工期时确实该偏资深，显务实。
  • 收尾： seniors 稳、juniors 长，呼应同学。

亮点表达 / 句型

  • a *mixed* roster（混合阵容，HR 类精准词）
  • hit the ground running（上任即上手，地道习语）
  • resist fresh methods（抵触新方法，点出全资深隐患）

写作思路（可借鉴点）

  1. 招聘类题高分答法是"组合拳"：不是二选一，而是"按阶段/风险分配"。
  2. 用 A good case in point from my lab 把例子锚定到自己背景，真实感强、不套路。
  3. 让步句 Admittedly … Still … 同时承认对方合理 + 补出盲区，最显成熟度。
