06 · 市中心该禁车吗？· 学术讨论写作范文

主题：城市规划 ｜ 难度：★★★☆☆ ｜ 目标字数：~155

题目

Professor: Some cities ban private cars from their downtown cores to cut pollution and reclaim public space. Is a full car ban a good policy, in your view? Why or why not?

Kelly (student): Yes—centers are calmer and cleaner when cars are gone. I love car-free zones when I visit.

范文

I'd agree with Kelly that car-free centers feel great, but I'd argue for limited restriction rather than a full ban.

The reason is trade-offs. A good case in point: when my city pedestrianized the main square, foot traffic and small businesses rose—exactly Kelly's point. Yet delivery vans, taxis, and drivers with disabilities still need access; a blanket ban hurts them and can push commerce to out-of-town malls.

Admittedly, the air-quality and safety gains Kelly enjoys are real and valuable. The smarter fix is time-and-zone limits: ban private cars at peak hours, allow clean transit and essential vehicles, and invest in reliable buses so people aren't stranded.

Unlike Kelly, who supports an outright ban, I'd stress pairing restrictions with alternatives so the policy lasts.

中文解析

结构拆解

  • 第 1 句：接 Kelly + 立"限行而非全禁"的折中立场。
  • 理由 + 例子：用"广场步行化"真实案例证成好处，同时点出配送/无障碍/外溢购物等代价。
  • 让步：承认 Kelly 说的清洁安全是真收益。
  • 收尾：限制须配替代方案才可持续，呼应同学。

亮点表达 / 句型

  • limited restriction rather than a full ban（限行而非全禁，政策类精准立场）
  • blanket ban（一刀切禁令，贬义精准）
  • pairing restrictions with alternatives（限制配替代，政策可持续性核心）

写作思路（可借鉴点）

  1. 城市规划题极易考"极端政策"，高分答法是"支持目标、反对一刀切"，显政策素养。
  2. 例子用"本地亲历的步行化广场"，具体且有对比（好处 vs 代价）。
  3. outright ban（彻底禁令）与 limited restriction 对照，是这类题的得分词汇对。
