听后复述句库（Listen and Repeat）

新版口语第一大题，共 7 题，听一句（无文字）完整复述，录音 8–12 秒。 本句库为文字版训练材料：用任意 AI 语音 / 真人朗读生成音频后练习，或先默读再遮住原文复述。 训练要点：抓主干（主+谓+宾）优先，再补形容词/副词/介词短语；录完回听漏了什么成分。

Level 1 · 短句（8–12 词）

  1. The library closes at nine on weekdays.
  2. She decided to study abroad next year.
  3. We missed the bus and walked home instead.
  4. His presentation was clear and easy to follow.
  5. The new cafe on Maple Street is surprisingly good.
  6. They canceled the meeting because of the storm.
  7. My roommate fixed the sink without calling anyone.
  8. Students must submit the form by Friday afternoon.

Level 2 · 中句（13–18 词）

  1. Although it was raining, they went for a long hike in the hills.
  2. The professor asked us to read two chapters before the next class.
  3. I realized I had left my phone on the table near the window.
  4. Our team finished the project a week early and won the top prize.
  5. She explained that the delay was caused by a shortage of staff.
  6. If you arrive before noon, you can join the guided tour for free.
  7. The movie was longer than we expected, but worth every minute.
  8. He suggested we meet at the station so no one would get lost.

Level 3 · 长难句（19+ 词）

  1. The professor explained that the experiment would be postponed until further notice from the lab.
  2. Despite the heavy snow, the volunteers managed to deliver food to the isolated village.
  3. What surprised most visitors was how quietly the ancient temple sat among the trees.
  4. The committee agreed the new policy should take effect at the start of next semester.
  5. Because the train was delayed, we barely caught the last ferry across the bay.
  6. She reminded him that the application, once submitted, could not be changed online.
  7. The survey found that people who walked daily reported better sleep than those who did not.
  8. Even though the instructions looked simple, assembling the shelf took the whole afternoon.
