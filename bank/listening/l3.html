听力 Transcript L3 · Student Service 对话

文字稿练习素材，配合官方/免费音频使用（见资源页）。 训练目标：说话人需求 + 解决步骤 + 态度变化。

Clerk: Hi, how can I help you today?

Maria: Hi, I'm an exchange student and I locked myself out of the campus housing portal — actually, I think my account got flagged. It says "access suspended" when I try to log in.

Clerk: Okay, don't worry, that happens. Usually it's a password try too many times, or a hold on your record. Let me check your student ID. ... Right, there's a hold — it's from the health center. You haven't submitted the immunization form they emailed you in August.

Maria: Oh! I never saw that email. I was still in my home country then. Is that why I can't pick my dorm?

Clerk: Exactly. The portal stays locked until the hold clears. But it's an easy fix. Go to the health center's website, download the form, and upload your vaccine record. Once they mark it received, the hold drops within a day, and you can log back in.

Maria: Great, that sounds simple. Can I do it from my phone right now?

Clerk: Sure — but make sure the scan is readable; blurry uploads get rejected and that restarts the wait. If it's accepted, you'll get an email, then try the portal again tomorrow morning.

Maria: Perfect, thank you so much. I was worried it was something serious.

Clerk: Nope, just paperwork. Happy to help.

题目

Q1

题干：Why can't Maria log in to the housing portal? 选项： A. She forgot her student ID number B. A health-center hold blocked her account C. The system is down for maintenance D. She typed the wrong password too many times 答案：B 解析：Clerk 查后说"there's a hold — from the health center"，因未交免疫表，对应 B。A 未提；C 未提；D 是 Clerk 说的"usually"可能原因之一，但本案是 hold。

Q2

题干：What caused the hold, according to the clerk? 选项： A. An unpaid tuition bill B. A missing immunization form from August C. A failed math exam D. A roommate complaint 答案：B 解析：Clerk 明确："you haven't submitted the immunization form they emailed you in August"，对应 B。A/C/D 均未提及。

Q3

题干：What does the clerk tell Maria to do to fix the problem? 选项： A. Visit the health center in person tomorrow B. Download, fill, and upload the vaccine form online C. Call the IT help desk to reset her password D. Pay a penalty fee at the bursar 答案：B 解析：Clerk 给步骤：去 health center 网站下载表格、上传疫苗记录，对应 B。A 错（线上即可）；C 错（非密码问题）；D 未提。

Q4

题干：What warning does the clerk give about the upload? 选项： A. It must be submitted before August B. Blurry scans get rejected and restart the wait C. Only PDF format is accepted D. It can only be done on a school computer 答案：B 解析：Clerk 提醒"make sure the scan is readable; blurry uploads get rejected and that restarts the wait"，对应 B。A/C/D 均不符。
