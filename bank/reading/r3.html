阅读 Passage R3 · 失败博物馆

练习文本（ETS 风格，非官方真题）。训练目标：主旨题 + 否定事实题 + 作者目的。

Most museums celebrate what succeeded. The Museum of Failure, with locations in several cities, does the opposite: it displays commercial flops, rejected prototypes, and products that companies would rather forget. A frozen yogurt shampoo, a soda for dogs, a phone with a keyboard on the back — each item carries a small label explaining not just what it was, but why it died. The point, the curators insist, is not mockery but education.

The exhibits make a serious claim: failure is data. When a product flops, the reason is often visible in hindsight — a misread market, a feature nobody asked for, a price set by hope. By studying these corpses in public, the museum argues, organizations can train their instincts before spending millions on the next bad idea. One display pairs a failed tablet with the successful one that learned from it, suggesting that the flop was tuition, not waste.

Critics note the limits. A museum can romanticize failure, implying that any stumble is virtuous. In reality, some failures are just careless, and celebrating them risks confusing recklessness with experimentation. The curators answer that the labels are unsentimental: they name the specific mistake, not a vague "we tried." The honesty, they say, is the whole value.

Whether or not the lesson sticks, the crowds keep coming, drawn partly by the comedy of a cat-shaped toaster that never shipped. Yet many visitors leave with a quieter takeaway: the things we hide as mistakes are often the most informative. A society that displays its failures, the museum whispers, might just learn faster than one that buries them.

题目

Q1

题干：The primary purpose of the Museum of Failure is to 选项： A. mock companies that waste money B. educate visitors by studying failed products C. sell rejected prototypes at low prices D. compare old and new technology 答案：B 解析：首段末句策展人强调"not mockery but education"，第二段进一步说失败是数据、可训练直觉，对应 B。A 是策展人明确否定的；C/D 未提及。

Q2

题干：According to Paragraph 2, why does the museum show a failed tablet next to a successful one? 选项： A. To prove the failed one was better designed B. To suggest the success learned from the failure C. To show prices have fallen over time D. To criticize the company's marketing 答案：B 解析：第二段末：把失败平板与成功的并置，说明"the flop was tuition, not waste"——成功是从失败中学来的，对应 B。A/C/D 均不符。

Q3

题干：Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a reason products fail? 选项： A. A misread market B. A feature nobody requested C. A price set by hope D. A factory fire 答案：D 解析：第二段列举失败原因：misread market（A）、feature nobody asked for（B）、price set by hope（C）。factory fire（D）全文未提，选 D 为否定事实题答案。

Q4

题干：How do the curators respond to critics who say failure is romanticized? 选项： A. They remove the funny exhibits B. They say labels name the specific mistake honestly C. They argue all failures are virtuous D. They close the museum on weekdays 答案：B 解析：第三段：策展人回应称标签"unsentimental"，点名具体错误而非模糊的"我们试过了"，即诚实指出具体失误，对应 B。A/C/D 均不符文意。

Q5

题干：What is the "quieter takeaway" (Paragraph 4) many visitors leave with? 选项： A. Cat toasters are funny but useless B. Hidden mistakes are often the most instructive C. Museums should charge higher entry fees D. Successful products are always copied 答案：B 解析：末段："the things we hide as mistakes are often the most informative"——被藏起来的错误往往最有教益，对应 B。A 是吸引人流的喜剧因素非 takeaway；C/D 无据。
